James McMath

Software Developer

About

James McMath is a software and game developer passionate about staying up-to-date with the latest trends in technology, and is always looking for new ways to enhance his skills and expertise in all technologies. He has worked as a Unity developer for over 2 years, building XR applications, games, and data visualizers.

James having worked several years as an IT support agent has assisting customers with various technology issues in both software and hardware, utilizing a vast range of tools and services to help streamline processes and notate user/client changes. Outside of work, James strives to support his community in the best ways he can, helping to educate people and assist non-profits in growing and maintaining their presence.
